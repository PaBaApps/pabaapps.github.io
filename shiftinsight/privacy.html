2. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is processed when using the mobile application ShiftInsight – Shift Planner and its related online features.

ShiftInsight is designed for personal shift planning and evaluation, including working times, premiums, estimated earnings, vacation, sickness, holidays and, if enabled, sharing selected shift information with a partner.

3. Principles and legal bases

Personal data is processed only to the extent necessary to provide the features you use. We apply principles of data minimisation, purpose limitation, storage limitation and appropriate technical and organisational safeguards.

  • Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR: providing the app, user accounts, synchronization and requested features.
  • Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR: where processing is based on your voluntary consent.
  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: legitimate interests, particularly secure and stable operation and abuse prevention.
  • Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR: where processing is necessary to comply with a legal obligation.

4. Local data storage

A substantial part of ShiftInsight data is stored locally on your device using local app storage, including Hive.

Depending on how you use the app, locally stored data may include shift/calendar entries, custom shifts and colors, shift patterns, working-time and wage settings, premium rules, vacation and sickness entries, manual holidays, partner shift entries, language and display settings, and technical identifiers needed for cloud/partner features.

Local storage alone does not transmit this data to PaBaApps or a cloud provider. Data is transferred only when you use the online, account or synchronization features described below.

5. User account and email sign-in

ShiftInsight supports accounts using an email address and password. Accounts are used to identify users and to provide cloud-based functionality, restoration and synchronization.

Registration and sign-in may process your email address, a unique user ID (UID), authentication information and technical login data. PaBaApps does not store your password in plain text; authentication is provided through Google Firebase Authentication.

6. Firebase Authentication

ShiftInsight uses Firebase Authentication by Google for registration, sign-in and password reset.

According to Google, Firebase Authentication may process data including email addresses, passwords, IP addresses and user-agent information for authentication, account management, security and abuse prevention.

Google states that Firebase Authentication is operated from data centers in the United States. More information: Firebase – Privacy and Security and Google Privacy Policy.

7. Cloud synchronization with Cloud Firestore

ShiftInsight uses Google Cloud Firestore / Firebase Firestore for certain account, cloud and partner features.

Depending on the features used, Firestore may store or synchronize:

  • shift entries and related calendar data,
  • shared-calendar membership,
  • partner shift entries within a shared calendar,
  • manually created holidays,
  • partner invitation and link information,
  • selected account-related app settings intended for restoration or synchronization,
  • technical timestamps and identifiers required for synchronization.

Cloud synchronization is used to make authorized calendar data available on eligible devices, synchronize partner calendars and restore supported settings after signing in again.

Important: Not every locally stored preference is automatically backed up to the cloud. Only data intended for the relevant synchronization feature is transmitted. Payment/Premium information and internal entitlement data are not treated as freely editable general user settings.

8. Shift and calendar data

ShiftInsight processes information you enter or import for your personal shift planning, such as shift type, date, start/end time, breaks, vacation, sickness and labels or notes you choose to add.

This data is used for calendar display, statistics, working-time and earnings calculations and, where enabled, synchronization.

ShiftInsight does not automatically send your shift, working-time or earnings data to employers, authorities, insurers or other third parties.

9. Partner feature and linking

ShiftInsight provides a partner feature that allows two users to connect their shift calendars. Linking is performed using the email address of the intended ShiftInsight partner account. ShiftInsight does not automatically send an email message as part of this linking process.

Data processed for this feature may include:

  • unique user IDs of the linked accounts,
  • the email address of the invited or intended partner account,
  • technical calendar and linking identifiers,
  • invitation or link status,
  • shift and calendar information intended for use in the shared calendar.

If both users already have calendar data before linking, existing data may be merged into a shared calendar as part of the partner-linking process. Where different entries exist for the same person and calendar day, entries created by that person are generally preferred. Partner-entered information may be used to fill gaps where the person has no own entry for that date.

The email address is used only to identify or connect the intended ShiftInsight account. Linking a partner does not automatically share unrelated local device or display preferences.

10. Holidays and premium rules

ShiftInsight can take public/configured holidays and manually created holidays into account. Manual holidays may form part of synchronized calendar data when cloud or partner features are used.

The app may also store locally configured values for night, late, Sunday or public-holiday premiums and other compensation settings for your personal calculations.

11. Earnings and working-time data

ShiftInsight may process hourly wage, shift times, break times, premium percentages, vacation entitlement and calculated values in order to display working time, premiums and estimated earnings.

Values shown in the app are personal planning/calculation values and do not replace an official payslip, tax advice or legal employment advice.

12. Google Play and Premium purchase

ShiftInsight may offer optional paid features through Google Play Billing. Payment processing is handled by Google Play.

PaBaApps does not receive your full credit-card or bank-account details. Technical purchase/product information may be processed to unlock or restore a purchase, for example product ID and purchase status.

Google is independently responsible for processing data within Google Play. See the Google Privacy Policy.

13. Feedback and email contact

If you use a feedback/contact feature, ShiftInsight may open the email application installed on your device with pre-filled recipient, subject or message information.

No email is sent until you confirm sending it in your email application. The privacy rules of your email provider then apply.

If you contact PaBaApps by email, we process the information you provide only to respond to your request and conduct necessary follow-up communication.

14. No advertising and no independent tracking

Under its current feature set, ShiftInsight does not use advertising SDKs or features intended to create advertising profiles.

Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore are used for authentication, security, synchronization and partner features, not by PaBaApps to track users across unrelated apps or websites for advertising purposes.

16. International data transfers

When Google/Firebase services are used, personal data may also be processed outside the European Union or European Economic Area, particularly in the United States.

Google describes mechanisms for international transfers including the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and contractual data-protection arrangements. Further information is available on Google's Firebase privacy pages.

17. Data security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encrypted HTTPS/TLS transmission, authenticated accounts and access controls for cloud data.

According to Google, Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication encrypt data in transit and support encryption of stored data.

18. Retention

Local app data generally remains on the device until it is deleted in the app, removed during certain account changes, or the app/app data is deleted from the device.

Cloud data is retained for as long as needed for account, synchronization or partner features or until deletion, unless legal obligations require longer retention. Technical authentication data is also subject to Google/Firebase retention periods.

19. Sign-out, account deletion and data deletion

Signing out may remove account-bound local calendar data from the device while cloud data can remain associated with the account so it can be restored after a later sign-in. Signing out is therefore not the same as deleting the account.

If an in-app account deletion option is not yet available in the app version you use, you can request deletion of your ShiftInsight account and associated personal cloud data by emailing pabaapps@web.de.

For connected partner calendars, deletion may affect shared data and link information. Where technically and legally possible, personal associations will be removed without deleting the other partner's data without authorization.
